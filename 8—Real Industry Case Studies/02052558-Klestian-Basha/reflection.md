# Personal Reflection

While researching this topic, I realized that AI is becoming a normal part of software engineering instead of something that will completely replace developers. What surprised me most was how many leading companies already use AI every day to help their engineering teams.

I think AI creates great opportunities because it saves time on repetitive work and allows developers to focus on more creative and complex tasks. At the same time, I believe software engineers must understand the code they write instead of blindly trusting AI-generated solutions.

This research also made me realize that I should continue improving my programming fundamentals, problem-solving skills, communication, and knowledge of software design. I plan to use AI as a tool to help me learn faster while always checking and understanding the results myself.

Overall, my opinion became more positive after this research. I believe the future belongs to software engineers who know how to combine technical knowledge with AI tools responsibly.
